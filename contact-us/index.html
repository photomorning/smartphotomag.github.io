Get In Touch

Send editorial notes, corrections, product coverage ideas, partnership inquiries, and advertising requests to the Smart Photo Mag desk.

Editorial

We welcome useful tips, corrections, source notes, and article ideas related to AI photography tools, editing workflow, camera technology, posing guides, creator resources, and practical photo education. If you spot something that should be clarified or updated, include the article title and the specific section you are referring to.

Email editorial messages tosupport@smartphotomag.com.

Business

For sponsorships, product announcements, review sample proposals, affiliate partnerships, or media inquiries, contact us with a short summary of your brand, the product or campaign, the preferred timing, and the type of placement you are interested in.

Send business messages tosupport@smartphotomag.com.

What To Include

Clear context helps us respond faster. Please include your name, company or publication if relevant, the page URL or article title, and any supporting links. For product coverage, mention whether the tool is available now, in beta, or scheduled for a future release.

Response Time

We read incoming messages regularly, but response times can vary based on editorial schedules and publishing deadlines. We prioritize corrections, privacy requests, and time-sensitive business inquiries.
