10:T139b,<p>Facebook may not be the dominant social network it once was, but for millions of users, it still holds years of irreplaceable photographic memories — weddings, graduations, births, and vacations buried in old profile albums. Fortunately, Facebook provides a straightforward, built-in tool to download your entire photo library in one batch. Below is a quick guide to pulling your photos off the platform, whether you&#039;re on a computer or a mobile device.</p>
<img src="https://shotkit.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/download-your-photos-from-facebook.jpg" alt="A Facebook user downloading their photo archive onto a laptop" loading="lazy" />
<h2>Download All Your Photos in One Batch (Computer)</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Log into Facebook</strong> and click your profile picture in the top-right corner.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Settings &amp; Privacy</strong>, then click <strong>Settings</strong>.</li>
<li>In the left-hand sidebar, click <strong>Your Facebook Information</strong>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Download Your Information</strong>.</li>
<li>Click <strong>Deselect All</strong>, then scroll down and check only the box labeled <strong>Photos and Videos</strong>.</li>
<li>Choose your preferred <strong>Date Range</strong>, <strong>Format</strong> (HTML is easiest to browse), and <strong>Media Quality</strong> (set to High for the best image resolution).</li>
<li>Click <strong>Request a Download</strong>. Facebook will compile your archive — this can take several minutes or hours depending on the size of your library — and notify you via email when it is ready.</li>
<li>Once the file is ready, you will receive a notification and can click the download link to save the ZIP archive to your computer.</li>
</ol>
<img src="/media/articles/how-to-download-all-your-photos-from-facebook/01.avif" alt="Download Facebook photo archive settings page showing selection screen" loading="lazy" />
<h2>Download Photos Using the Facebook Mobile App</h2>
<p>The process is essentially identical on a smartphone. From the Facebook app:</p>
<ol>
<li>Tap the <strong>Menu</strong> (hamburger icon).</li>
<li>Scroll to <strong>Settings &amp; Privacy</strong> and tap <strong>Settings</strong>.</li>
<li>Under <strong>Your Information</strong>, tap <strong>Download Your Information</strong>.</li>
<li>Follow the same steps as above: deselect all, check only <strong>Photos and Videos</strong>, set your quality to High, and request the download.</li>
<li>Your archive will be compiled by Facebook&#039;s servers. Take care to download it over a stable Wi-Fi connection, as the ZIP file can be large.</li>
</ol>
<h2>How to Download a Single Photo or Album</h2>
<p>If you only need a few specific images rather than your entire library, you can avoid the full archive download:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Single Photo:</strong> Open the photo, click the three-dot menu in the top-right corner, and select <strong>Download</strong>.</li>
<li><strong>Entire Album:</strong> Open the album, click the three-dot menu, and select <strong>Download Album</strong>. Facebook will compile all images from that album into a single ZIP file for you.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Practical Tips</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Use a computer for large archives.</strong> Downloading a decade&#039;s worth of photos over a mobile connection can eat through data caps and take hours.</li>
<li><strong>Sort and back up immediately.</strong> Once downloaded, organize your files into cleanly labeled folders (such as by year or event) and back them up to an external hard drive or a secure cloud storage solution.</li>
<li><strong>Choose High quality.</strong> The default medium compression can leave photos looking noticeably soft. Always select the High quality option before requesting the download.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Final Thoughts</h2>
<p>Downloading all your photos from Facebook is a simple but critical step in taking ownership of your digital memories. Backing them up locally and then storing a second copy in a cloud service or on an external drive ensures that even if a platform changes its policies or an account becomes inaccessible, your photographic history remains safe in your hands.</p>
<h2>FAQ</h2>
<p><strong>How long does it take for Facebook to compile my photo archive?</strong> It depends on the size of your library. Most archives are ready within 30 minutes to a few hours. Facebook sends an email notification and an on-platform alert when the file is prepared, and the download link remains active for several days.</p>
<p><strong>Can I download photos that other people have tagged me in?</strong> Yes. The download tool includes photos uploaded by others where you are tagged. These images may be slightly lower resolution than the originals, as Facebook compresses files upon upload.</p>
<p><strong>What format do the photos download in?</strong> Your photos are packaged into organized HTML folders in a ZIP file.
