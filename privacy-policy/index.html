Privacy Policy

Your Privacy

This page explains the basic privacy practices for Smart Photo Mag, including analytics, advertising, and external photography resources.

Overview

Smart Photo Mag is a content website about photography, editing, AI imaging tools, camera technology, and creator workflows. You can browse the site without creating an account. We aim to collect only the information needed to operate, improve, protect, and support the website.

Information We Collect

We may collect standard analytics information such as page views, referring pages, device type, and approximate location when analytics tools are enabled.

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your name, email address, company, message, links, attachments, or article references. We use that information to respond to your request, review corrections, consider business inquiries, and maintain appropriate records.

Cookies And Analytics

The site may use cookies or similar technologies for analytics, performance measurement, security, advertising, affiliate tracking, or embedded third-party services. Analytics may help us understand which articles are useful, how readers find the site, and which pages need improvement.

Advertising And Links

Advertising partners or external links may use their own cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics systems. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Articles may link to photography tools, software companies, retailers, image platforms, social networks, and other external resources. Once you leave this site, the destination website is responsible for its own privacy practices, terms, cookies, and data handling.

Affiliate And Sponsored Links

Some pages may include affiliate links, sponsored placements, or commercial references. If affiliate links are used, the partner may receive information needed to attribute a referral or purchase. Sponsored or advertising relationships do not give partners access to private email messages sent directly to us.

Image Sources And Embedded Media

Some articles may include images, embeds, or references from third-party photography resources. These services may receive limited technical information when their content loads in your browser, such as your IP address, browser type, or referring page.

Data Retention

We keep contact emails and business records for as long as needed to respond, manage editorial work, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain normal business records. Analytics data retention depends on the settings of the analytics provider in use.

Your Choices

You can disable cookies through your browser settings, use browser privacy controls, or avoid clicking external links if you do not want third-party sites to receive referral information. You can also contact us to request correction or deletion of personal information you have sent directly to us, subject to legitimate recordkeeping needs.

Children's Privacy

This site is intended for a general photography audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Policy Updates

We may update this privacy policy when the website, advertising practices, analytics tools, or legal requirements change. The most recent version will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, emailsupport@smartphotomag.com.
